# Changelog

Notable changes to MudiVoLTE. The version is in `package/VERSION`; the
packages and their checksums are on the
[releases page](https://github.com/xarvel/MudiVoLTE/releases).

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-14)

First public release: calls and SMS on the GL.iNet Mudi 7 through Linphone
on the router's Wi-Fi, encrypted calls (SIP over TLS, SRTP) with an option
to close the unencrypted SIP ports, pushes for phones in the background
through subscribe.linphone.org, pages in the GL admin panel and LuCI, calls
away from home over Tailscale.
